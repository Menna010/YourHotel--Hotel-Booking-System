About the Resort

Comfortable Accommodations: Palma Resort in Hurghada offers family rooms with air-conditioning, private bathrooms, and balconies. Each room includes bathrobes, free toiletries, and a work desk.

Exceptional Facilities: Guests can enjoy a spa and wellness center, sauna, hot tub, and waterpark. The resort features a terrace, garden, restaurant, bar, and free WiFi. Additional amenities include a 24-hour front desk, electric vehicle charging station, and free on-site private parking.

Prime Location: Hawaii Beach is a 5-minute walk away, and Hurghada International Airport is 15 mi from the property. Reception staff speak Arabic and English.
